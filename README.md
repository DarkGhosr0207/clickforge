# CTRLab

**CTRLab** is an **AI YouTube CTR tool**: it helps you invent stronger thumbnails and titles, score directions, and get clear recommendations—before you spend time in Canva.

## Who it’s for

YouTube creators and teams who care about **click-through rate** and want repeatable ways to test ideas, not one-off guesses.

## What it generates

- Thumbnail **concepts** and directions for a given video idea  
- **CTR-oriented scoring** and a recommended top pick  
- Optional **image previews** and downloads for your chosen variants  

## Why it’s useful

You iterate in one place on **what might get clicked**, instead of jumping straight into design with no hypothesis—so you ship thumbnails aligned with what your audience responds to.
